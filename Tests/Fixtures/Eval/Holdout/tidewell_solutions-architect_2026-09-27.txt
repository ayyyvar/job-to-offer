Solutions Architect
Location: National Capital Region
Workplace: Hybrid
Employment: FullTime

The Solutions Architect is responsible for designing, developing, and implementing technical solutions that align with business objectives. This role bridges the gap between business requirements and technical capabilities: ensuring that solutions are scalable, reliable, and efficient down to implementation.




JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

Technical Leadership

 - Designing and architecting complex software systems.

 - Ensuring adherence to SDLC, IT policies and standards, industry best practices

 - Oversee development of Solutions from inception to release 

 - Ensure regulatory compliance for solutions that are crafted 

 - Develop comprehensive technical architectures for new products, features, or systems

 - Evaluate and select appropriate technologies, platforms, and frameworks

 - Ensure that solutions adhere to best practices, standards, and guidelines

 - Create detailed technical designs, including data models, system diagrams, and component specifications

 - Provide guidance to development teams on implementation details

 - Review and approve technical designs

 - Identify and troubleshoot technical issues
   

IT Project Management

 - Plan and manage software development projects, ensuring they meet deadlines 

 - Collaborate with product owners and stakeholders to define requirements and prioritize features

 - Oversee the entire software development lifecycle, from design to deployment
   
   


ROLE REQUIREMENTS

Core Competencies

 - In-depth experience of Agile software Development • Experience with software architecture design and system integration

 - Hands-on experience on working on distributed systems and microservices 

 - Experience with Stakeholder management 

 - Experience with software architecture design and system integration

 - Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills 

 - Hands-on experience in building application pipeline 

 - Hands-on experience in coaching and leading a small team of engineers

 - Knowledge in core banking systems is a plus (ThoughtMachine, Oradian)
   

Qualifications

 - Graduate of any 4 year IT related course

 - At least 6 years total working experience 

 - Working experience in financial or banking industries

 - Good to have: AWS SA, or any devOps Certification
