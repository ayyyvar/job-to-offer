About the role:

You'll build the analytics layer for Finance and Marketing & Growth — two areas that only get partial coverage today — and help shape how analytics engineering scales as the team and data volume grow.

What you'll do:

 - Build the semantic layer that powers Finance's financial reporting, turning business logic into reliable, gold-layer datasets.

 - Build the analytics layer for Marketing & Growth, enabling self-service reporting on acquisition, funnels, and campaign performance.

 - Help define how analytics engineering scales across the team — supporting other analysts' data models and shaping technical standards.

What you'll own:

 - Own the semantic layer for financial reporting, ensuring one consistent source of truth for business metrics.

 - Own the analytics layer for Marketing & Growth, from data modeling to self-service reporting.

 - Build and maintain dbt models (staging, intermediate, marts) with tests, documentation, and reusable macros.

 - Design scalable data models in Databricks silver and gold layers, applying dimensional modeling.

 - Support other analysts across the data team, optimizing self-built data models when performance or reliability issues come up.

 - Contribute to defining the strategy and standards for analytics engineering as it grows.

What makes you a strong fit:

 - Strong SQL expertise, including complex queries, window functions, and query optimization on large datasets

 - Hands-on experience with dbt — models, macros, tests, documentation, and CI workflows

 - Strong dimensional modeling experience (3NF/Inmon, star schema, or anchor modeling)

 - Experience building semantic layers or maintaining a single source of truth for business metrics

 - Experience in banking or fintech is a plus, especially data modeling for financial products

 - Comfortable working in Git-based ELT workflows
