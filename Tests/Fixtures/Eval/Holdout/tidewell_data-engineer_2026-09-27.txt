About the role

You'll build and own the data pipelines and lakehouse architecture that turn raw data into reliable inputs powering products used by millions of people.

What you'll do

 - Design and run batch and streaming pipelines on Databricks and Apache Spark over AWS, ingesting from databases, APIs, and event streams.

 - Shape our lakehouse architecture on S3 and Delta Lake, including medallion (Bronze / Silver / Gold) layering and storage optimization.

 - Work directly with analytics, engineering, and product teams to turn raw data into reliable, usable inputs for the business.

What you'll own

 - Own projects end to end — from stakeholder alignment and design review through implementation, launch, and ongoing support.

 - Build self-service data solutions that let other teams deliver their own data needs, designed to scale and extend easily as new sources and use cases are added.

 - Build and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines that are fault-tolerant and cost-optimized.

 - Optimize Spark jobs end to end — cluster sizing, caching, joins, shuffles, partitioning, and storage formats (Parquet, Delta).

 - Manage Databricks Workflows, jobs, cluster configurations, and the underlying infrastructure — security policies, resources, and access — responding to failures and performance issues.

 - Implement data governance and access control with Unity Catalog across accounts and environments, including handling of PII.

What makes you a strong fit

 - Hands-on experience with Databricks and strong proficiency in Apache Spark (PySpark and/or Scala).

 - Strong Python for data engineering, plus SQL and Spark SQL for transformation and analytics.

 - Experience with Delta Lake and lakehouse architectures, including performance optimization.

 - Proven ownership — you've run projects where you knew the weak points, gathered feedback, and made your solutions easy to scale and maintain.

 - Experience writing clean, testable, maintainable code with Git-based version control.

 - Experience with infrastructure-as-code such as Terraform or Ansible is a plus — not required, and easy to pick up on the job.
