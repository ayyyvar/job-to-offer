About the role:

You'll build the middleware connecting Tidewell's ATM fleet to backend systems, as we scale from a pilot network to thousands of machines across the Philippines.



What you'll do:

 - You'll work on real-time transaction flows between ATM hardware and Tidewell's backend, handling authorization, reversals, and reconciliation as the fleet scales from pilot to thousands of machines.

 - You'll integrate directly with an HSM for PIN translation, key management, and cryptographic operations — core to how every transaction stays secure.

 - You'll work closely with hardware, infrastructure, security, compliance, and vendor teams (SmartDico, the processing switch) to keep the ATM stack running end to end.



What you'll own:

 - Design, build, and maintain backend services for the ATM middleware platform

 - Own ISO 8583 message flows between the ATM fleet and the processing switch, including authorization, reversals, and reconciliation

 - Integrate with the HSM for PIN translation, key management, and cryptographic operations

 - Build and maintain integrations with card schemes and the ATM middleware vendor

 - Improve APIs and system integrations across the ATM stack, including anti-fraud and core banking connections

 - Support production monitoring, incident investigation, and resolution for ATM transaction services



What makes you a strong fit:

 - 3+ years of experience as a Backend Engineer with production experience in Kotlin, ideally in fintech, banking, or payment processing

 - Experience with microservice architecture, API design, and building system integrations from scratch

 - Knowledge of ISO 8583 or similar financial messaging protocols

 - Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS/EKS), containerized deployments, and GitLab/GitOps CI/CD pipelines

 - Strong SQL skills for data analysis and validation

 - English level B2 or higher, with strong written and verbal communication
