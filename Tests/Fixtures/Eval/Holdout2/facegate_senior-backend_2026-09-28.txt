Senior Backend Biometric
Facegate
850,000 to 1,200,000 KZT NET per month
Almaty / Office

The company develops enterprise KYC platforms and ML service ecosystems. The role involves enterprise KYC platforms and ML service ecosystems, including international biometrics and FinTech projects.

Responsibilities:
• Develop enterprise KYC platforms and ML service ecosystems.
• Create microservices for biometric authentication, including OCR, liveness detection, and face recognition.
• Integrate solutions with state systems and FinTech platforms.
• Develop on-premise solutions for corporate clients.
• Design and optimize high-load APIs.
• Participate in DevOps infrastructure, monitoring, and CI/CD setup.

Requirements:
• 3+ years of commercial Python experience.
• Excellent knowledge of Django, Django REST Framework (DRF), Django-Ninja, and FastAPI.
• Experience with AsyncIO, Aiohttp, and SQLAlchemy.
• Proficiency in PostgreSQL, Redis, and MongoDB.
• Experience with Celery and RabbitMQ.
• Containerization and orchestration skills using Docker, Kubernetes, and GitHub Actions.
• Experience with Prometheus, Grafana, and Loki monitoring systems.
• Experience writing tests with Pytest.
• Mentoring and code review skills.

Optional:
• Understanding and application of TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenCV, ONNX, and scikit-learn.
• Experience in KYC/AML, FinTech, or government integrations.
• Knowledge of biometric systems.
• Experience with performance optimization and high-load solution architecture.
