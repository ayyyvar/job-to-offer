AI Developer / ML Engineer
Pivotra
1,600 to 3,200 USD NET per month
Remote

Pivotra focuses on innovative AI/ML solutions. Developing an internal AI platform for intelligent agents.

Responsibilities:
• Design and build multi-agent systems
• Integrate LLMs with internal tools and services
• Develop backend services and APIs for agent workflows

• Person re-identification and vector search (Milvus / FAISS)
• Video processing: ffmpeg / GStreamer, RTSP, hardware decoding

Optional:
• NLP, ML experience

• Vision-language models (VLM)
