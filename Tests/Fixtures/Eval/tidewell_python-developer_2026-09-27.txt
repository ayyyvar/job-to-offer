ABOUT THE ROLE

You'll join the Python Development team to design and build new backend services, expanding engineering capacity so Tidewell can launch strategically important new service lines.


WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Design and build new backend services in a microservice, event-driven environment, with reliability as the primary constraint.

 - Coordinate architecture and integrations with the Core and RBB teams to keep cross-team delivery consistent.

 - Contribute to the delivery of a major new service line — for example collections or cross-matching graphs — as business priorities take shape.


WHAT YOU'LL OWN

 - Own key components in the existing architecture and take on new service domains as they come up.

 - Design service architecture and choose communication patterns between services.

 - Instrument services with metrics for monitoring and alerting, and cover them with unit tests.

 - Identify and resolve technical debt and architectural bottlenecks.

 - Build and maintain shared libraries and tooling for recurring tasks.

 - Explore and adopt new instruments, and bring your own ideas to how we build.


WHAT MAKES YOU A STRONG FIT

 - 3+ years of production experience as a Python backend developer.

 - Strong Python: typing, testing, packaging, asyncio, threading, multiprocessing.

 - Production experience with microservice architecture.

 - Familiarity with event-driven systems (e.g. Kafka).

 - Experience with Docker and Kubernetes, preferably on AWS EKS.

Nice to have

 - Experience with AWS and Terraform, domain-driven design, a statically typed compiled language, work in finance, antifraud or risk, or graph databases and graph-based data models.
