🧩 About the Position

We are looking for a Senior AI / ML Engineer to work on a client project.

In this role, you will work at the intersection of Machine Learning and Software Engineering, helping move ML models from experimental stages into production environments and integrate AI solutions into working products.

Due to an NDA, we are not disclosing details about the company, client, or project at this stage. More information will be available after the initial CV review.

🚀 About the Role

The main focus of the role is to develop, implement, and support production-ready ML models and AI applications.

The AI / ML Engineer will work closely with Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and Product teams, combining Data Science and Software Engineering approaches.

🔧 Responsibilities

- Develop and implement production-ready Machine Learning models.
- Build and integrate AI applications.
- Move ML models from experimental stages into scalable production environments.
- Work on integrating AI capabilities into products.
- Collaborate with Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and Product teams.
- Combine ML development with software engineering practices.

🎯 Requirements

- Experience in AI / ML Engineering.
- Experience developing and implementing Machine Learning models.
- Understanding of the process of moving models from experimental stages into production.
- Experience working with AI applications.
- Understanding of both Data Science and Software Engineering approaches.
- Experience collaborating with Data Science, Data Engineering, and Product teams.
- English — Upper-Intermediate+.

🎁 What We Offer

🚀 Remote / Full-time / Long-term collaboration

🤝 The opportunity to work on an international client project in a white-label format

💡 High level of ownership and autonomy in your work

🏖 21 paid vacation days + 10 paid sick leave days + Ukrainian public holidays

🗣 Individual English lessons

💊 Medical insurance (for employees based in Ukraine)

📚 Safari Books Online subscription

🧠 Corporate psychologist sessions

📋 Hiring Process

At the first stage, we collect CVs and submit them to the client for an initial review.

If your CV is approved, we will contact you and provide more details about the project, company, and next steps in the hiring process.

The specific hiring stages are not disclosed at this stage and will be shared after the CV is approved.
