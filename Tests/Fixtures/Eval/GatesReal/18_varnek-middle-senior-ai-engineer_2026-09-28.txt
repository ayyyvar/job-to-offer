Varnek Solutions is an international IT company specializing in web and mobile development for clients across the US, Canada, EU, and Australia. We are growing rapidly and are looking for a Middle\Senior AI Engineer to join our team and drive complex, high-impact projects.

Job Requirements:

- 3+ years of experience deploying machine learning models (classical ML and/or LLMs) in cloud environments (AWS/GCP/Azure) using containers and Kubernetes
- Hands-on experience with MLOps tools such as MLflow, Kubeflow, SageMaker, Vertex AI, Feast, or equivalent
- Proven experience building robust data/feature pipelines and implementing monitoring for model drift and performance
- Strong Software Engineering fundamentals: version control, automated testing, CI/CD, security, and cost-aware development
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills in English
- Ownership mindset, curiosity, and a bias for action in a fast-paced environment

Responsibilities:

- Design, implement, and maintain end-to-end ML pipelines for both classical ML and LLM-based solutions
- Deploy and manage models in cloud environments (AWS / GCP / Azure) using containers and Kubernetes
- Apply MLOps best practices for reproducibility, automation, and monitoring
- Build reliable data/feature pipelines and implement real-time monitoring for model drift, latency, and performance tracking
- Actively contribute to engineering excellence through CI/CD, testing, security, and cost optimization

Nice to have:

- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Understanding of distributed systems (Spark, Ray)
- Experience working with feature stores and data governance

We provide the following for our employees:

- Competitive salary according to qualification
- Comfortable working environment
- Experienced colleagues
- Opportunity to work with interesting projects,
- Regular performance reviews
- Friendly atmosphere, care for the comfort of professionals
