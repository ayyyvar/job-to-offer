About the Product

We are developing a platform of AI analyst coworkers that deliver industry insights. The agents operate in secure, sandboxed environments and can use tools, communicate across channels, and complete complex tasks with human oversight.

About the Role

You will join a small engineering team building and operating the platform’s agentic AI capabilities. This is a hands-on role covering AI agents, system architecture, production reliability, feature design and secure deployment.

Tech Stack

- TypeScript, Node.js, and Bun

- LLM APIs, agent SDKs, and MCP

- SQL and SQLite

- GCP, including Cloud Run, Cloud Build, and Artifact Registry

- Terraform and CI/CD

- Event-driven messaging and asynchronous processing

- Structured logging, monitoring, and tracing

Key Responsibilities

- Design and build production AI agents that can plan, use tools, maintain context, and complete complex tasks.

- Design scalable systems for agent orchestration, messaging, task execution, permissions, and persistent state.

- Integrate LLM providers, external APIs, databases, and communication channels.

- Build secure, sandboxed environments for executing agent workloads.

- Develop evaluation, monitoring, and debugging tools for agent quality and reliability.

- Deploy and operate AI services on GCP.

- Own technical decisions and establish strong engineering, testing, and security practices.

Required Skills

- 7+ years of software engineering experience.

- Hands-on experience delivering production AI or agentic systems used by real users.

- Strong system design experience, particularly with distributed and event-driven systems.

- Strong backend development skills in TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, Rust, or a similar language.

- Experience with LLM APIs, tool calling, structured outputs, context management, and agent workflows.

- Experience with SQL databases, asynchronous processing, retries, idempotency, and failure handling.

- Production experience with GCP, Docker, CI/CD, and infrastructure as code.

- Strong understanding of application security, permissions, secrets, and workload isolation.

- Experience with observability, incident investigation, and production operations.

- Ability to work independently and communicate clearly in a fast-moving environment.

Nice to Have

- Experience with agent frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK, or similar.

- Experience with Terraform, Kubernetes, vector search, RAG, or multi-agent systems.

- Experience with financial research or analyst workflows.
