job-to-offer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-12

job-to-offer keeps your data on your iPhone. We run no servers, collect no data, and use no tracking or analytics.

Accounts

job-to-offer has no account and no login. You do not give us an email address, a name, or a password.

Data we collect

We collect no data. The developer receives nothing from the app: no usage data, no crash reports, no identifiers, no analytics, and no advertising data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • your candidate profile (regions, languages, skills);
  • the resume text you import, if you import one;
  • your tracker (the jobs you saved and their statuses);
  • the posting text the app fetched or you pasted.

Deleting the app removes this data from your iPhone.

Network requests

The app makes one request for each posting you share or paste:

  • the URL you shared, to read the job posting;
  • for a Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby link, the matching public job API for that same posting.

The app does not crawl sites, does not scrape job boards, and does not contact any other service.

Parsing and analysis

The app reads the posting with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. The text does not leave your device for this step.

Export

The app exports your tracker only when you tap export. You choose where the file goes, for example Files or another app. The app does not send exports anywhere.

Children

job-to-offer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page changes too, and the effective date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/ayyyvar/jobscout/issues.

job-to-offer is open source. Source code: github.com/ayyyvar/jobscout.
