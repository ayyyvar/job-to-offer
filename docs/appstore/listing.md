## Description (4000 max)

JobScout tells you in seconds whether a job posting fits you. Share a posting from Safari, LinkedIn, or Telegram, or paste its link. JobScout reads the posting and checks the rules that matter most for a cross-border job search.

WHAT YOU GET
- Eligibility check: visa sponsorship, citizenship or residency requirements, and security clearance.
- Language check: whether the job requires a language you do not speak.
- Location check: remote, hybrid, or on-site rules against the regions you choose.
- Fit score: four weighted parts (technical skills, experience, behavior, career direction), a band from Strong Fit to Poor Fit, strengths, and gaps.
- Quoted proof: every FAIL or FLAG shows the exact sentence from the posting, so you can check it yourself.
- Tracker: keep each job with its status, from Drafted to Hired, and edit any field.
- Export: send your tracker to Files or another app as a spreadsheet file or as JSON.

PRIVATE AND FREE
- Free. No account, no subscription, no ads.
- Private. Your profile, resume, and tracker stay on your iPhone.
- Works offline once a posting is fetched. Parsing runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
- No tracking and no analytics.

REQUIREMENTS
- iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- English device language and English Siri language.
- English job postings only.

HOW IT WORKS
JobScout fetches the one posting you share. It reads the posting with Apple Intelligence and runs the checks in plain Swift code. The AI model never decides a PASS, FAIL, or FLAG. The score is an estimate. Treat a verdict as a starting point and read the posting yourself.
